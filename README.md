# Sales Data Exploratory Analysis

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Superstore retail dataset 
to uncover sales trends, discount impact, and top products.

## Tools Used
- Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Questions Answered
- Which Category has the Highest Sales ?
- Which Region Performs Best?
- What is the Monthly Sales Trend?
- Is there a relationship between Discount and Profit?
- Top 10 products by sales?

## Key Findings
- Technology category generates the highest revenue among all categories
- The West region contributes the most to overall sales (31.6%)
- Sales are growing year-over-year with strong seasonal peaks in Q4
- High discounts (above 40%) consistently result in losses — discount strategy needs revision
- Office technology products (copiers, binding systems) are the top revenue drivers
